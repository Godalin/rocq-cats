# Simply typed lambda calculus and cartesian closed categories

The signature is a type of base labels. Types are generated by unit,
products and function types. A term carries its typing derivation, and βη
equality carries its witness in `Type`. [Syntax](../../theories/STLC/Syntax.v) · [Equivalence](../../theories/STLC/Equivalence.v)

## Interpreting substitution

Choose a CCC and an interpretation of the base labels. A context becomes
a product of objects and a judgment becomes an arrow from that product.
Variables denote projections, pairing denotes the product mediator, and
abstraction and application use exponential transposition and evaluation.
Renaming and substitution act by precomposition. The substitution lemma
therefore reduces soundness of β and η to the universal properties of the
chosen products and exponentials. [Substitution](../../theories/STLC/Substitution.v) · [Semantics](../../theories/STLC/Semantics.v)

## The syntactic model and completeness

Contexts and substitutions form a category. Packing a context as its product
type relates the context and type presentations. Adding syntactic exponentials
gives a CCC in which interpretation recovers the original terms up to βη.
Interpretations into other CCCs extend the chosen base-type interpretation;
structure-preserving functors compare interpretations by natural isomorphism.
[Context packing](../../theories/STLC/Packing.v) · [Syntactic CCC](../../theories/STLC/CCC.v) · [Comparison](../../theories/STLC/Comparison.v) · [Free property](../../theories/STLC/Free.v)

Equational completeness uses the syntactic model among the models at a
common universe bound. Inhabitation completeness asks for an arrow with
the interpreted endpoints and recovers a term in the syntactic model.
The setoid model is a concrete instance for computing interpretations.
[Equational completeness](../../theories/STLC/Equational.v) · [Inhabitation](../../theories/STLC/Inhabitation.v) · [Setoid interpretation](../../theories/STLC/Set.v)

## Inhabitation / provability completeness

[STLC.Inhabitation](../../theories/STLC/Inhabitation.v)
defines `TmModelInhabited Γ A`, which supplies a morphism `CtxI I Γ → TyI I A` for
every CCC and base interpretation at exactly the common universe bounds of
`TmModelEq`. It is a uniform family of witnesses in `Type`, not a Prop-only
nonemptiness assertion. The two constructive directions are
`tm_inhabitation_soundness` and `tm_inhabitation_completeness`, bundled by
`tm_inhabitation_soundness_iff_completeness`:

```text
Tm Γ A ↔ ∀ (C : CCC@{b b e l l}) (I : B → Ob C),
            Hom_C (CtxI I Γ) (TyI I A)
```

Soundness evaluates the term with `tm_interp`. Completeness instantiates
the family with `TyCat_CCC B` and `ty_base`. The existing
`type_compare` and inverse `context_compare` maps for `IdF` turn the chosen
semantic morphism into a literal arrow `CtxTy Γ → A`. This arrow is a
singleton-context substitution; taking its `var_zero` component and
substituting `ctx_pack_sub Γ` gives a term in the original context.

This is inhabitation completeness for the truth, conjunction and implication
fragment under Curry–Howard–Lambek. It complements equational completeness;
the bundled maps assert neither equality of witnesses nor inverse laws.
No untyped syntax, normalization, decidable equality, or additional axiom
is required.

The flat modules [Equational](../../theories/STLC/Equational.v) and
[Inhabitation](../../theories/STLC/Inhabitation.v) share the syntactic CCC and
comparison theory; each states soundness and completeness for its own goal.
